[player_help tutorial/5588.htm|Back to the List] *New Character Bonus* The first character on each server to reach *level 6* is called a "new character". Before a character reaches level 6, another character can be created on the same server and developed into a new character. A new character receives various benefits up to level 25. The benefits given to a new character are as follows: *Exchange Equipment* Equipment can be exchanged in weapon, armor and accessory shops by paying the difference in price. All no-grade equipment can be exchanged in this way. *Assist Magic* The guides in every village will enchant new characters with assist magic matched to their level. New characters between levels 10 and 24 are enchanted with different assist magic depending on their level. *Additional Quest Bonuses* Additional bonuses are bestowed on new characters in various quests.
