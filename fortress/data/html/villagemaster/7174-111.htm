!Spell Singers are somewhat similar to Human Sorcerers, but with Elven sensibilities. They practice a potent form of elemental magic. Very fast during combat and more efficient and safe than other Mages. Most dreaded opponent during a siege. To become a Spell Singer, your level must be over 40 and you must collect the *Mark of the Scholar*, the *Mark of Life* and the *Mark of the Magus*.
