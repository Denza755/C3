!Each mark requires its own trial. The *Mark of the Scholar* is reserved for those who pass the *Trial of the Scholar*. You must be above level 35 to qualify. To take this test visit Magister Mirien in the temple of Dion Castle Town. The *Mark of Life* is given to those who pass the *Testimony of Life*. You must be above level 37 to take the test. To take this test visit Master Cardien of Dion Castle Town. And last, the *Mark of the Summoner* is reserved for those who pass the Test of the Summoner. You must be above level 39 to qualify.
