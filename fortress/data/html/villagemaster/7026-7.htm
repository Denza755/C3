!Grand Master Bitz: To become a Knight, your level must be at least 20, and you must pass the Test of the Knight. But as I told you, you cannot become a Knight here. You must first go out into the world and improve your skills. Therefore, you must go to *Gludio* to become a Knight. There is a ship bound for the village of Gludin departing from the port southwest of the village. Sail to the village of Gludin. When you arrive there, either *Grand Master Ramos* or *Grand Master Rains* can change your profession to Knight after testing your qualifications. You can find *Grand Master Rains* in the town of Gludio, which is to the east of the village of Gludin. They will tell you everything you need to know in detail about the Test of the Knight.
